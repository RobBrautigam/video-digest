## Summary

A NASA scientist answers the "podium test": what evidence would let a scientist stand before the world and say life has been found beyond Earth. The answer is a sequence of checks (is the signal real, is it contamination, could anything other than life make it) followed by independent lines of evidence and scrutiny by the whole scientific community.

## Methods

- **0:00:06** (NASA scientist) Frame the claim as a test: what would it take to stand before an international audience and say it?
  > "We call this the podium test"
- **0:00:31** (NASA scientist) Chemical signs of life have to stand out above non-living (abiotic) chemistry.
  > "detected above a background of abiotic chemistry"
- **0:01:46** (NASA scientist) Check one: are we sure we are seeing what we think we are seeing?
  > "Are we sure we're seeing what we think we're seeing"
- **0:02:19** (NASA scientist) Check two: rule out contamination from the Earth-built spacecraft and instruments.
  > "not evidence of our own life, but evidence of indigenous life"
- **0:02:29** (NASA scientist) Check three: could anything other than life make the same thing, including processes not yet known?
  > "is there any other way than life to make that thing"
- **0:02:47** (NASA scientist) Look for separate, independent lines of evidence that corroborate the first one.
  > "do we have separate independent lines of evidence"
